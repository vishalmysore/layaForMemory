What is scored

Strict: the verdict equals the label. Lenient: a stale fact (superseded, contradicted, needs review) gets any of those three, a standing fact (confirmed, unrelated) gets either of those two, and questions and commands are recognized. False invalidation: a fact that still holds is marked superseded or contradicted, the one mistake the policy is built to avoid.
